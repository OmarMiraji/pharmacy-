PharmSpecio — SOMA KWANZA (Windows)

PharmSpecio.exe SI "Setup" / installer.
Ni programu yenyewe — ikifunguka mara moja bila wizard, hiyo ni kawaida.

Kusakinisha kwenye PC (Program Files + shortcut Desktop):
  → Bofya mara mbili:  Install-PharmSpecio.bat
  → Kubali "Run as administrator"
  → Subiri ujumbe "Done"
  → Fungua PharmSpecio kutoka Desktop

Usihamishi PharmSpecio.exe peke yake — lazima folder nzima (dll + data).
