Mfumo mmoja, kazi zote za duka la dawaOne system for the whole pharmacy

Kuanzia dawa inapoingia dukani mpaka inapouzwa — PharmSpecio inakumbuka kila hatua.From the moment stock arrives until it is sold, PharmSpecio keeps track of every step.

🧾

Mauzo ya haraka (POS)Fast point of sale

Tafuta dawa, ongeza kwenye kikapu, weka punguzo na uchapishe risiti kwa sekunde chache. Cash, M-Pesa au mkopo.Search, add to cart, apply discounts and print a receipt in seconds. Cash, mobile money or credit.

💊

Stock na batchesStock and batches

Kila dawa na batch yake, bei ya kununua na kuuza. Pata tahadhari stock ikifika kiwango cha kuagiza.Every medicine with its batches, buying and selling price. Get alerts when stock reaches the reorder level.

⏳

Ufuatiliaji wa expiryExpiry tracking

Ona dawa zinazokaribia kuisha muda mapema, uziuze kwanza au uzirudishe — punguza hasara.See medicines close to expiry early so you can sell or return them first and cut losses.

🚚

Manunuzi na wasambazajiPurchases and suppliers

Rekodi oda, pokea mzigo na stock ijiongeze yenyewe. Jua unadaiwa na nani na kiasi gani.Record orders, receive deliveries and stock updates itself. Know what you owe each supplier.

👥

Wafanyakazi na ruhusaStaff and permissions

Kila mfanyakazi ana login yake. Wewe unaamua nani auze, nani abadilishe bei, nani aone ripoti.Each staff member gets their own login. You decide who sells, who edits prices and who sees reports.

📊

Ripoti na faidaReports and profit

Mauzo, manunuzi, matumizi na faida kwa siku, wiki au mwezi. Toa ripoti kama Excel au PDF.Sales, purchases, expenses and profit by day, week or month. Export to Excel or PDF.

📶

Hufanya kazi bila mtandaoWorks offline

Mtandao ukikatika, endelea kuuza. Taarifa zinajituma zenyewe mtandao ukirudi.If the internet drops, keep selling. Everything syncs automatically when you are back online.

🤖

Msaidizi wa AIAI assistant

Uliza kwa Kiswahili: "Panadol imebaki ngapi?" au "Mauzo ya leo ni kiasi gani?" — upate jibu papo hapo.Ask "How much Panadol is left?" or "What are today's sales?" and get an answer right away.
